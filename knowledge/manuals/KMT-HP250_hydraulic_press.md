# KMT HP-250 hydraulic press

## Overview
The KMT HP-250 is a 250 t hydraulic press with coil feeder for stamping and forming brackets.
Main assemblies: hydraulic power unit (tank 400 l, pump, oil cooler with fan, filters), main
cylinder, die area with quick die clamps, servo coil feeder, safety light curtain and two-hand
control. Machine in this plant: PRESS-01.

## Safety before any maintenance
Never reach into the die area while the press is in operation. Before die changes, cleaning
or repairs, stop the press, lower the ram onto safety blocks and apply lockout/tagout
(SOP-LOTO), including the hydraulic accumulator (release stored pressure). The light curtain
and the two-hand control must never be bypassed, muted or bridged. A faulty safety device is
repaired by qualified personnel; the press stays locked out until it is repaired and tested.

## Alarm H-104 Hydraulic oil temperature high
**Meaning:** Oil temperature in the tank is above 58 C (warning). Above 65 C the press stops
until the oil has cooled down below 55 C.
**Typical causes:** dirty oil cooler fins, slow or failed cooler fan, high ambient temperature,
low oil level, internal leakage (worn pump or valves heat the oil).
**Troubleshooting:**
1. Check the oil level in the sight glass.
2. Check that the cooler fan runs at full speed; a slow fan points to a worn fan motor bearing.
3. Inspect the cooler fins; clean them with compressed air from the clean side.
4. Compare the oil temperature at full load with the baseline (about 45-48 C).
5. If temperature stays high with a clean cooler and working fan, check the pump and relief
   valve for internal leakage.
**Note:** Repeated H-104 stops in summer are a sign of a dirty or under-performing cooler, not
only of the weather. Letting the oil cool down treats the symptom only.

## Alarm H-110 Hydraulic pressure low
**Meaning:** System pressure does not reach the set value during the stroke.
**Troubleshooting:** check for external leaks (hoses, fittings, cylinder seals), check the oil
level, check the pressure relief valve setting, check the pump. After any hose replacement,
bleed the system and perform a pressure test.

## Alarm H-220 Coil feeder jam
**Meaning:** The feeder did not reach the feed length within the time limit, or the strip
buckled.
**Troubleshooting:**
1. Lock out the press and the feeder. Remove the buckled strip section.
2. Check strip alignment with the strip guide, especially after a coil change.
3. Check feeder roller pressure and roller wear; worn rollers (FR-8) slip.
4. Check the strip lubrication.

## Die change procedure
Standard die change on PRESS-01 with quick die clamps: target time 35 minutes.
1. Prepare: next die, clamps, tools and first-part inspection gauges are at the press BEFORE
   the press stops (external setup).
2. Stop the press, ram to top position, release quick die clamps.
3. Move the die out on the die cart, move the new die in, center it on the locating pins.
4. Clamp, set shut height from the die card, set feeder length and program number.
5. Run 5 single strokes, check the first part with the gauge, release for production.
Startup rejects after a die change are normal (about 20-30 parts until the strip and
die are stable).

## Reducing die change time (SMED)
Single-Minute Exchange of Die (SMED) separates internal setup (press stopped) from external
setup (press running). Typical improvements:
- Prepare die, tools and gauges during production (external setup) using a setup checklist.
- Use the die cart and pre-set shut height cards for every die.
- Standardise clamp positions so no measuring is needed.
- Make sure the night shift has the same preparation support as the day shift (crane driver,
  die cart, setup team). Long night-shift die changes are usually caused by missing
  preparation or by waiting for help, not by the press.

## Preventive maintenance plan
**Daily (operator):** check oil level, check light curtain function with the test rod, check
two-hand control, lubricate strip.
**Weekly (maintenance, about 120 min):** check hoses and fittings for leaks, check filter
clogging indicator, check quick die clamps, check feeder rollers.
**Monthly:** clean oil cooler fins, check cooler fan speed, take oil sample in summer months.
**Yearly:** oil analysis and change if needed, accumulator pre-charge check, safety inspection.
