# SOP Lockout/tagout (LOTO)

## Purpose and scope
Protects people from unexpected start-up or release of stored energy during maintenance,
repair, cleaning and setup. Applies to all machines. Every person working inside a machine
attaches their own lock.

## Steps
1. **Prepare:** identify all energy sources (electrical, hydraulic, pneumatic, mechanical,
   gravity, stored pressure). Inform the operator and shift leader.
2. **Stop** the machine with the normal stop.
3. **Isolate** every energy source: main switch off, compressed air valve closed, hydraulic
   pressure released, ram or moving parts secured (e.g. safety blocks on the press).
4. **Lock and tag:** each worker attaches a personal lock and a tag with name and date.
5. **Verify zero energy:** try to start the machine; check pressure gauges read zero.
6. Do the work.
7. **Restore:** remove tools, refit guards, check that everyone is clear, each person removes
   their own lock, test safety devices, then restart.

## Safety devices are never bypassed
Light curtains, door interlocks, two-hand controls and emergency stops must never be bridged,
muted, jumpered or disabled to keep production running. A faulty safety device means the
machine stays stopped until the device is repaired and tested by qualified personnel.
Bypassing a safety device is a serious violation and must be reported to the shift leader.
